# NoMaD Workshop

Telegram content tools, built by NoMaD. Everything runs in your browser and videos are never uploaded.

**Open the app:** https://nomad-wolf-clan.github.io/Workshop/

No install, no account, no uploads. Open the link on a computer or phone and start creating.

## Tools

**BGRemover:** remove a solid-color background from a video or image.
- Video exports: Telegram video sticker (512 px, ≤3 s, ≤30 fps, ≤256 KB, VP9 with transparency), Telegram custom emoji (100×100), GIF, transparent WebM
- Image exports: transparent PNG, Telegram static sticker (512 px PNG), Telegram static emoji (100×100 PNG)
- Export several formats at once, then download each or all as a zip

**Upscale:** enlarge an image or video 2× or 4× with sharp resize.
- Images: PNG, WebP or JPG (up to 8192 px)
- Video: WebM (VP9), keeps audio (up to 4096 px)
- Split view compares the result against a plain resize

**GIF maker:** turn a video into a GIF.
- Resolution, frame rate, speed, colors and a target file size
- Optional background removal using your BGRemover key

## How to use
1. Choose a tool at the top, then **Open** a file.
2. Adjust the settings in the right-hand panel.
3. Click the main button (Export / Upscale / Make GIF) and download your files.

## Good to know
- Works best in Chrome or Edge on desktop or Android. Export isn't supported in every browser.
- Telegram turns GIFs into silent MP4s and drops transparency. For transparent Telegram content, use the sticker or emoji exports.
- iPhone videos are often HEVC and may not open in Chrome. Convert them to H.264 MP4 first.
