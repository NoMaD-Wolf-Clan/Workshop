# NoMaD Workshop

Telegram content tools, built by NoMaD. Everything runs in your browser and your files are never uploaded.

**Open the app:** https://nomad-wolf-clan.github.io/Workshop/

No install, no account, no uploads. Open the link on a computer or phone and start creating.

## Tools

**BGRemover:** remove a solid-color background from a video or image.
- Pick the exact background color with the eyedropper, or let the app detect it
- Tune Tolerance, Softness, Choke, Despill and Shadows, and check edges in Split or Matte view
- Video exports: Telegram video sticker (512 px, ≤3 s, ≤30 fps, ≤256 KB, VP9 with transparency, no audio), Telegram custom emoji (100×100, 64 KB by default, 256 KB optional), GIF, transparent WebM
- Image exports: transparent PNG, Telegram static sticker (512 px PNG), Telegram static emoji (100×100 PNG)
- Clips longer than 3 s are sped up to fit (or trimmed), cropped to your subject, and sized to stay under Telegram's limits
- Export several formats at once, then download each or all as a zip

**Upscale:** enlarge an image or video 2× or 4× with sharp resize.
- Images: PNG, WebP or JPG (up to 8192 px)
- Video: WebM (VP9), keeps audio (up to 4096 px)
- Split view compares the result against a plain resize, and 100% zoom shows real pixels

**GIF maker:** turn a video into a GIF.
- Resolution, frame rate, speed, colors and a target file size
- Optional background removal using your BGRemover key

**Editor:** edit a single clip.
- Split the timeline at the playhead into segments, delete the parts you don't want, and undo any change
- Set the speed of each segment (0.25× to 4×), with pitch-preserved audio
- Frame crop by dragging a box, with Free, 9:16, 1:1, 4:5 or 16:9 locks
- Change the aspect ratio (9:16, 1:1, 4:5, 16:9, 4:3) with bars, blurred background or fill
- Timed captions you type, burned into the video (size and position)
- Export MP4 (H.264) or WebM (VP9) at 1080p, 720p, 480p or source size, with audio

## Editor shortcuts
- **S** split at the playhead · **Delete** remove the selected segment · **Ctrl+Z** undo

## How to use
1. Choose a tool, then **Open** a video (MP4, WebM) or image (PNG, JPG, WebP).
2. Adjust the settings in the tools panel.
3. Click the main button (Export / Upscale / Make GIF) and download your files.

## On your phone
- Upright: the video stays at the top, tools scroll below, and the tool tabs sit in a bar at the bottom.
- Sideways: the screen splits in half, with the video on the left and tools on the right. It switches automatically when you turn the phone.

## Good to know
- Works best in Chrome or Edge on desktop or Android.
- iPhone and iPad (Safari): loading, background removal, GIF maker and image tools work. Video exports need iOS 16.4 or later, and some formats may not be available.
- Telegram turns GIFs into silent MP4s and drops transparency. For transparent Telegram content, use the sticker or emoji exports.
- Videos recorded on iPhone are often HEVC and may not open in Chrome on a computer. Convert them to H.264 MP4 first.
